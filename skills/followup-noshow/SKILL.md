# Follow-Up & No-Show Recovery — Cause Before Mechanics

The iron rule: NO NAKED REMINDERS. Every follow-up must give a researched, persona-specific reason the meeting is worth their time BEFORE mentioning the hold. A message that only says "the hold is still yours, one click" is banned.

## Structure of every follow-up

1. 1-2 sentences of true, researched value hypothesis: what their company does + what this persona owns + the specific gap your product addresses for THAT combination.
2. Then the mechanics: the slot, and the three-way choice - accept, propose a new time on the invite, or decline and it disappears.

## The windows

- **Mid-life follow-up**: hold is 2-3 business days old, still unanswered. One cause-first note in the existing thread.
- **Morning-of confirm**: meeting is today and still unaccepted. One short confirm by ~3 hours before. If still silent 1-2 hours out, delete the hold silently (no notification) and log `expired`. Never let them get a reminder for a meeting they never agreed to.
- **3+ unanswered touches total**: stop. Status `nurture`. No follow-up, no exceptions.

## No-show recovery (they accepted, then didn't join)

1. Wait 10 minutes on the call, then leave. Same day, send one note in the thread: assume good faith ("looks like the day got away, happens to everyone"), restate the one-line value, offer two specific new slots.
2. If they rebook: headline it, treat as booked.
3. If silence for 3 business days: one more note with a genuinely different angle (new information, not a re-ask). Then nurture.
4. Log every step in the repository. Recovered no-shows are the highest-converting segment; treat them warmly, never passive-aggressively.

## Reschedule requests

Always say yes gracefully. Move the hold to their proposed time, confirm in one line, done. If they name a week not a time, propose two specific slots in that week.
